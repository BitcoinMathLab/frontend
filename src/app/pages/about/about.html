Bitcoin should be inspectable.

Bitcoin Math Lab is an education and developer platform for exploring the mechanisms behind Bitcoin—without hype, financial predictions, or opaque black boxes.

Mission

Make the machinery visible.

We turn scripts, transactions, bytes, stack values, and consensus decisions into interactive experiences that people can examine at their own pace.

Approach

Explain the mechanism first.

The project favors concrete execution over analogy. Technical terms are introduced when they help describe something a learner can already see.

One product, three focused repositories.

  1. FrontendThe public Angular learning experience.
  2. BackendProduct APIs and orchestration around the labs.
  3. BitCloneReusable execution, consensus, tracing, and analysis.
  4. Bitcoin CoreThe production blockchain source of truth.
